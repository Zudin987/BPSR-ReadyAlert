v1.16.3 implementation notes

Protocol references used:
- CN Resonance class-spec canonical skill signatures (including Heavy Guardian Earthfort 1922 / Block 1930).
- ATTR_SKILL_LEVEL_ID_LIST (0x74) full player snapshots for pre-combat metadata and equipped Battle Imagine canonical IDs/remodel levels.

Behavior:
- infer spec before damage only when SkillLevel list evidence is unambiguous;
- retain combat-skill fallback for late/missing snapshots;
- full SkillLevel lists replace the authoritative Imagine pair; partial lists may update known slots but cannot invent a third slot;
- class fallback colors are used even before specialization is known;
- DPS row height 33 px with darker/more muted row colors.
